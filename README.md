# IBM-HR-Analytics & Attrition Analysis 
Google Data Studio Project

An end-to-end data analytics and verification project examining employee attrition drivers at IBM. This repository contains the raw HR dataset, visual dashboard insights, and strategic recommendations to improve employee retention.

---

## Executive Summary

Employee attrition directly impacts organizational stability, productivity, and hiring costs. This project evaluates an IBM HR dataset of **1,470 employees** to identify primary attrition risk factors and validate report findings against raw transactional data.

### Key Metrics Summary
* **Total Workforce:** 1,470 employees
* **Total Attrition Count:** 237 employees
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** **16.12%** (16.1% in report)
* **Average Monthly Income:** **$6,502.93** (~$6.5K)
* **Average Employee Age:** **36.92 years** (~36.9)
* **Workforce Gender Split:** 60.00% Male (882) / 40.00% Female (588)

## 📈 Key Attrition Drivers & Deep Insights

### 1. Age & Career Stage Vulnerability
* Employees in the **18–25 age bracket** exhibit the highest attrition rate (**35.77%**), followed by **26–35** (**19.14%**).
* Attrition plummets to **9.19%** for employees aged 36–45 as career stability increases.

### 2. Income Compensation Factors
* Employees earning **under $3,000/month** suffer a **28.61%** attrition rate.
* Higher compensation directly correlates with retention: employees earning **over $10,000/month** have an attrition rate of only **8.90%**.

### 3. Business Travel Stress
* **Frequent Travelers** experience a **24.91%** attrition rate (69 out of 277), compared to **8.00%** for non-travelers. Frequent travel is a major risk multiplier.

### 4. Department & Gender Dynamics
* **Sales Department** has the highest overall attrition rate (**20.63%**).
* **Human Resources** shows a significant gender discrepancy: **Female HR employees** suffer a **30.00%** attrition rate (6 of 20), whereas Male HR employees sit at **13.95%**.

### 5. Job Satisfaction Impact
* Low job satisfaction (Level 1) leads to **22.84%** attrition compared to **11.33%** for Level 4 (Very High).

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations for HR Leadership

1. **Early Career Mentorship & Career Pathing (18–25 Age Group):**
   * Establish structured career development, mentorship, and clear promotion timelines to retain young talent.
2. **Compensation Review for Entry/Junior Roles:**
   * Adjust baseline salaries for employees earning under $3,000/month to realign with industry benchmarks.
3. **Frequent Travel Relief & Flexible Work:**
   * Implement travel rotations and hybrid working arrangements for frequent business travelers to mitigate burnout.
4. **HR Department & Diversity Support:**
   * Conduct stay interviews with female HR personnel to identify root causes behind the 30.00% attrition rate.
